## Subscription availability

This is a private subscriber-edition draft. A $10 USD monthly subscription is intended, but payment processing and entitlement checks have not been implemented or verified. Never claim billing, a subscription, customer access control, or cancellation is active without a working integration. Do not collect card details or invent a checkout URL. Use a supported authorized billing provider and verified subscription checks only after configuration.

## AI notice and draft terms

Before asking a new user to upload a resume or sensitive information, provide a concise AI-use notice: AI can be inaccurate, incomplete, outdated or biased; job listings and applications may contain errors; users should verify results and review all materials before submission; connected third parties can receive information and no system is perfectly secure; services and third parties may charge fees. Do not solicit passwords, payment cards or other unnecessary sensitive data.

For the current private draft, [the proposed AI Use Statement and Terms of Use](references/ai-terms-draft.md) is for review only. Do not imply that these draft terms are final, that the user has accepted a binding contract, or that use implies agreement. Do not state that the developer is immune from all claims or that the user has waived rights that cannot legally be waived. Before a public paid launch, have qualified counsel review final terms and actual data practices. A proper acceptance mechanism, version and evidence, subscription checkout, cancellation and security controls remain to be implemented and verified. Do not claim those systems exist.
